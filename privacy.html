Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Cinemate ("we", "us", "the app") is a movie and TV tracking app. It has no accounts and no advertising; some or all of it is part of Cinemate Plus, an optional subscription. This policy explains what data is involved, why, and how it is handled. If you have questions, contact us at konansulx@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • No account, no sign-in. Cinemate never asks for your email address, phone number or password. There is nothing to register and no profile to create.
  • Your setup answers and name. When you first open the app you can tell it what to call you, which streaming services you use and which genres, films and shows you like. These answers are stored on your device. Your services, genres and country are sent with the requests to TMDB that pick films for you. Firebase Analytics receives them only as categories and counts: what you mostly watch, how often, and how many services, genres, films and shows you picked. Your name never leaves the device and is used only to greet you.
  • Your library stays yours. Everything you track — watchlist, watched titles, favorites, your star ratings and personal notes — is stored locally on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it also syncs through your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database) so it appears on your other devices. This data goes to Apple's servers under your Apple ID. We have no access to it, we operate no server of our own, and we never receive a copy.
  • Usage and device data. Through Google Firebase Analytics we collect app-interaction events (for example: which screens are opened and from where, which section of a screen and which position in a row was tapped, when a title is added to a list, rated or marked watched, which setup steps were seen or skipped, which Cinemate Plus offer and plan were shown or chosen, how a paywall was opened and closed, whether a purchase went through, with the product and its price, and when content failed to load), plus device model, operating-system version, app version, language and approximate region derived from IP at country level, and a random app-instance identifier. This is not linked to your identity. Your name and the text you type in search are never sent.
  • Subscriptions. The app uses Superwall to decide which Cinemate Plus offer to show and to know whether Plus is active. Superwall receives a random identifier for this installation, the device's vendor identifier, the app and iOS versions, which paywall was shown, and, from Apple, the state of the subscription: trial started, renewed, cancelled or refunded. See Superwall's privacy policy.
  • Notifications. Cinemate uses local notifications only — they are scheduled and delivered by your device. There is no push server and no device push token is collected or transmitted.
  • What we never collect. No photos, no camera or microphone access, no contacts, no precise location, no health data, no advertising identifier and no payment details: purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

2. How we use your information

  • To keep your tracked titles available and in sync across your own devices.
  • To show you movie and TV information you request.
  • To send the local reminders you have allowed.
  • To show the right Cinemate Plus offer and to know whether your subscription is active.
  • To understand how the app is used, in aggregate, so we can improve it.

3. Service providers

Cinemate relies on the following third parties. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — supplies all movie, TV, cast and artwork data. When you browse or search, your requests are sent to TMDB's API. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. See the TMDB privacy policy.
  • JustWatch — "where to watch" streaming availability shown in the app is provided by JustWatch through TMDB.
  • Apple (iCloud / CloudKit) — stores and syncs your library in your own private iCloud database. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Apple (App Store) — handles Cinemate Plus payments, renewals and refunds. We never see your name or payment details.
  • Superwall — chooses the Cinemate Plus offer and tracks the state of the subscription, as described in section 1. See Superwall's privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — usage analytics, as described in section 1. See Firebase privacy information.
  • News publishers. The News tab shows headlines from public RSS feeds. Opening an article leaves Cinemate and takes you to the publisher's own website in your browser, where that publisher's privacy policy applies.

4. Data retention

Your library data stays on your device and in your iCloud account for as long as you keep it — we cannot delete it for you because we cannot reach it. Analytics and subscription data are retained for a limited period in line with Firebase's and Superwall's retention settings and are not tied to your identity.

5. Your rights and deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. To also remove the synced copy, delete Cinemate's iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. For any question or request about this policy, email konansulx@gmail.com.

6. Children

Cinemate is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will address it.

7. Security

Your tracked data is protected by your device's own protections and by Apple's iCloud security. Network requests to TMDB use encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. International transfers

The providers listed above may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. By using the app you consent to this processing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be reflected in the app or here.
